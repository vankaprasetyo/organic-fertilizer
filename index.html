Food Waste Organic Fertilizer

by Laras, Keysha, Alcuino, Vanka, & Aufa

Turning daily kitchen scraps into rich, sustainable soil.

The Background

Food waste is a huge environmental issue everywhere. Every day, homes, markets, and restaurants throw away tons of fruit peels and leftover veggies. In landfills, this waste rots and releases harmful greenhouse gases. Meanwhile, synthetic chemical fertilizers damage long-term soil health.

The Solution: Composting turns food waste into nutrient-rich soil via beneficial microorganisms, reducing landfill waste and providing an eco-friendly, budget-friendly alternative for plant care.

Materials & Ingredients

🍎 Food Waste
🪴 Soil / Coco Peat
🪣 Buckets (2x)
🔪 Knife
💧 Water
🚰 Dispenser Tap
🧫 EM4 Bacteria Solution

Step-by-Step Method

1
Drill a 5 cm hole near the bottom of the first bucket, install a water tap, and seal it to prevent leaks.
2
Cut the center out of one bucket lid to leave only the outer rim, then drill small drainage holes across the bottom of the second bucket.
3
Place the cut lid rim onto the lower bucket, stack the perforated bucket on top, and set the unit on a raised stand.
4
Mix 1 L of water and 20 mL of EM4 Agriculture solution inside a spray bottle.
5
Add a base layer of rice husk ash, raw rice husks, coco peat, or soil inside the upper bucket.
6
Chop kitchen waste (vegetables, fruit scraps, eggshells) into small pieces and add a layer inside.
7
Cover waste with husk ash/husks, spray bioactivator solution until moist, and close the top lid.
8
Repeat the waste, husk layer, and spraying process as new kitchen scraps are added.
9
Liquid Harvest: Collect liquid organic fertilizer (leachate) from the tap after 2–4 weeks; expose to sunlight in a clear bottle before use.
10
Solid Harvest: Allow solid waste in the upper bucket to decompose fully for 2–3 months before harvesting solid compost.

Results & Impact

🌱 Solid Compost

Improves soil structure, aeration, and long-term water retention for healthiest root growth.

💧 Liquid Fertilizer

Provides readily available nutrients for fast plant absorption and growth enhancement.
